:::tldr
Tarn & Vale is a two-person letterpress in the Douro that prints wine labels and wanted a mark of its own. We drew one, cut it in metal so it prints on their machine, and designed the box it ships in from a single sheet. The type came second and the press came first.
:::

## The constraint

Everything the press makes goes through a 1962 Heidelberg platen. So the wordmark had to be a thing that could be cast or cut in metal, printed with one impression and one colour, and still look like itself on damp label stock. A drawing that only works on a screen would have been a drawing for someone else.

::figure{src="/media/tarn-hand.webp" alt="A hand drawing letters on a sheet of paper on a cutting mat, with pens and a ruler beside it" caption="The first drawings, on the mat. The ampersand took a week; the rest of the letters took two days." width="wide"}

## The face

One serif, one weight, uppercase and lowercase, figures and an ampersand. It is drawn with a deep ink trap at every junction so the letter stays open when the press is over-inked, which on a platen it always slightly is.

:::callout{kind="note" title="Why one weight"}
A press with one machine and two people does not need a bold. It needs a face that looks the same at 8 pt on a label and at 36 pt on the box, and a second weight would have been a second set of metal to cast and keep.
:::

## The box

One sheet of 400 gsm board, die-cut and folded, no glue. The mark is blind-embossed on the lid, which the press does with the same plate it prints from, with the ink taken off.

::figure{src="/media/tarn-packaging.webp" alt="Kraft paper envelopes tied with string, a stamp and a pair of pliers laid out on a wooden table" caption="The box, the stamp and the string. Everything on this table is made or printed in the same room."}

:::steps{title="How a box is made" time="4 min"}
1. **Cut** — one pass through the die on the platen.
2. **Emboss** — the lid goes back through with the mark plate and no ink.
3. **Fold** — five creases, no glue; the tabs hold it.
4. **Tie** — string and a stamp, by hand, because it is a letterpress and they wanted to.
:::

:::pullquote{cite="Joana Tarn, printer" href="/posts/tarn-and-vale/"}
We had a face on the shelf that could print anything. We did not have one that could print us.
:::

## Questions

:::faq
### Can I license the face?
No. It was drawn for the press and the metal is theirs. Write to them; they take commissions in it.

### Is there a digital version?
There is, for the website and for setting the labels before they are cast. It is the same drawing. It is not for sale.

### Why an ampersand?
Because there is one in the name and the one on the shelf was wrong.
:::
