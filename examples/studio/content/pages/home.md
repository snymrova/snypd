We take a product from the first sketch to the first thousand units, and we build the jigs, the tests and the tooling on the way. Most studios hand that part to someone else. We think it is the part.
:::

## How we work

:::stat-row
::stat{value="31" label="products shipped since 2017" source="https://ferrule.snypd.rocks/about/#by-the-numbers"}
::stat{value="640 m²" label="of workshop, one floor" source="https://ferrule.snypd.rocks/about/#the-floor"}
::stat{value="11 weeks" label="median, brief to first batch" source="https://ferrule.snypd.rocks/about/#by-the-numbers"}
:::

:::steps{title="Four stages, one room"}
1. **Look** — two weeks in the client's world before anyone draws: the shop floor, the kitchen, the ward. We come back with what the object has to survive.
2. **Draw and break** — the first prototype is cardboard, the fourth is machined, and we break every one of them on the rig before you see it.
3. **Tool** — the fixtures, gauges and test jigs that let a factory make the thing the way we drew it. We keep the drawings; you keep the tools.
4. **Ship** — we stand next to the line for the first batch and write the manual the operators actually read.
:::
