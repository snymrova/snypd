The clearest description of the problem we found was written about something else entirely.

> The cost of a document is not its size on disk. It is the work a reader has to do to find the part they came for. A document that is small and badly organised is more expensive than one that is large and well signposted. Every structural element you add is a promise about where things are, and every promise you keep saves the reader a search.

We rewrote the primitive list twice after reading that.
