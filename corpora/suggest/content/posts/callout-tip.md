---
title: Reading the bench record faster
date: 2026-04-29
tags: [benchmarks]
---

The bench record is sixty rows and nobody reads sixty rows.

> Tip: read the Status column first. A row that says `report` has no budget and is there for the next decision, not this one; the rows that can fail the build all say ✅ or ❌.

The notes are the second pass, and only for the rows that moved since the last run.
