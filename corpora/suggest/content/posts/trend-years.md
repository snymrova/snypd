---
title: Ten years of page weight
date: 2026-03-22
tags: [benchmarks]
---

The median page has grown every year we have data for, and the growth is almost entirely script.

| Year | Kilobytes |
| --- | --- |
| 2016 | 890 |
| 2018 | 1420 |
| 2020 | 1810 |
| 2022 | 2140 |
| 2024 | 2390 |
| 2026 | 2610 |

Our content pages ship twenty-seven kilobytes and no script at all, which is not a boast so much as a description of what you get when nothing is allowed to opt in.
