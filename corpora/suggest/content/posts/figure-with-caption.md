The review page is the one screen a human sees before a draft becomes a post.

![The review page: the rendered draft on the left, its markdown twin on the right, one approve button](/media/review.png)

*The approve button applies to this exact version — editing the draft afterwards invalidates it.*

It is deliberately one page and one button, because approval is a decision and not a workflow.
