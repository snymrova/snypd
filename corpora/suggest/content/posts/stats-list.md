The release notes are long. These are the three lines from them that changed how we work.

- 92 % fewer tokens per page ([bench](https://snypd.rocks/bench))
- 504 tokens for a median post ([bench](https://snypd.rocks/bench))
- 0 KB of JavaScript on a content page ([bench](https://snypd.rocks/bench))

The last one is the one we defend hardest, because it is the one that is easiest to lose by accident.
