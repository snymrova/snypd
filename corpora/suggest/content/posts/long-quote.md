The notes from the first review are worth reading in full, so here is the paragraph that mattered.

> The proposal is coherent but it is not yet a product. A product has a first five minutes, and this one starts with a config file. Show me the first post, not the schema. Show me what an agent reads before it writes. Then show me the number that says the read was cheap. If those three exist, the rest of the document is implementation detail.

Three sessions later, those three things existed.
