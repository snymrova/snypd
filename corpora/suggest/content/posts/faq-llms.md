The same four questions arrive every week, so here they are with the answers we actually have.

### Does llms.txt help ranking?

No search engine has announced support for it, and we have measured no difference on our own corpus.

### Does anything help?

A markdown twin served on `Accept: text/markdown`. Every agent we tested already sends that header.

### Should I remove the file?

There is no cost to leaving it. There is a cost to believing it does something.

### What would change our mind?

A published statement from a crawler that reads it, or a measurable difference in a controlled test.
