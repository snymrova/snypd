The easiest way to see the difference is to put the two files side by side.

![Side-by-side view of the HTML source and the markdown twin of the same post](/media/twin.png)

*The twin on the right is the file the author wrote; the HTML on the left is what a crawler gets.*

Everything the agent needs is in the right-hand pane, and it is a twelfth of the bytes.
