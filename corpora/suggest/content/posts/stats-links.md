The gallery lane photographs every look and measures it first. Three numbers came out of the first run.

- 0 KB of JavaScript on every look ([gallery record](https://snypd.rocks/bench))
- 30.43 KB of webfont against a 31 KB declaration ([gallery record](https://snypd.rocks/bench))
- 0 axe violations across twelve renders ([gallery record](https://snypd.rocks/bench))

The second is the one to watch: the declaration is the theme's own, and a face that grows past it fails the lane.
