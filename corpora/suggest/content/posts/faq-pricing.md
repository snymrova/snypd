date: 2026-04-23

A short set of answers about money, because the design set has a position and it should be findable.

### Is the free tier complete on its own?

Yes. It is the product. Anything paid is a service around it, never a feature held back.

### Will a custom domain ever cost money?

No. On a static artefact behind a CDN a domain costs the provider nothing, and charging for it would be a hostage rather than a service.

### Is there metered analytics?

No. Rent on someone else's traffic is the loudest complaint about every hosted CMS, and there is no reason to inherit it.
