A lightly edited exchange from the review, kept as a dialogue because the back-and-forth is the point.

**Q.** Why does the kill test score the finished site and not the transcript?

**A.** Because the transcript is the model's and the site is ours. A route can be strange and still arrive.

**Q.** Then why count the calls at all?

**A.** Because a route that arrives in forty calls is a surface that made the model work for it, and that is a finding about the surface.

**Q.** And the three models?

**A.** The same test, three drivers, so the surface is the constant.
