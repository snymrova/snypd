The things worth checking before a draft goes live, in no particular order.

- The description is under 160 characters
- Every tag is one the site already uses
- Every stat has a source that resolves
- The cover image has alt text
- The preview was opened at a phone width

None of these are steps. They are things that are either true or not, and the lint says which.
