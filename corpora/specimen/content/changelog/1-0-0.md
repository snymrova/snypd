The column is fixed now. A theme may change its measure and its type, but not the grid a figure breaks out of, and every page in this corpus was re-read against it. Anything a theme drew outside the grid before this version is a bug the theme owns.

## What breaks

Blocks take their props from one contract. Three props were renamed on the way — a callout's `kind` is its `tone`, a stat's `n` is its `value`, and a figure's `wide` is `breakout` — and the old names are errors, not warnings, so a post that used them says so at build rather than rendering the wrong thing.

## What stays

Every post in the corpus renders as it did, and the feed validates against both readers it was tested in.
