Most posts are not a tour of the vocabulary. They are headings, paragraphs, a list, a link and a code
span, and a theme that only looks right when a post is full of blocks is a theme that looks wrong most
of the time.

## Body copy

The measure, the leading and the space between a heading and the paragraph under it are
the whole design at this size. Everything else is decoration on top of a column of text.

- A list item, because lists are half of technical writing
- A second one, to show the gap between them
- A [link](/about/) and a `code span`, which are the two things prose does that plain text cannot

## A quote

> Long-form reading. One serif column at a comfortable measure, generous leading, a single
> accent used sparingly.
