# The judge's rubric

You are looking at photographs of a page you wrote. Read them the way somebody who did not write them
would: not "is this what I meant", but "what does this look like". The six axes below are the ones a
rendered page fails on while every gate is green.

**This never gates** (decision 224). It is advice from a second pair of eyes; the gates are
`check theme` and `bench page`, and the choice is the owner's.

## How to look

1. Read the **sheets**, not the theme. One sheet per route and scheme: the candidates side by side, each
   at 390 · 768 · 1280 · 1440. The comparison is the point — a fault you cannot see in one column is
   usually visible the moment a neighbour does it better.
2. Look at **dark before light**, and at **390 before 1440**. Both are the halves that get designed last
   and looked at least.
3. Name a **region**, never a vibe. "The entry list's dates at 390, dark" is a finding. "It feels
   cluttered" is not, and cannot be acted on.
4. Propose **at most five changes**, each one a token or a rule, each naming the shot it came from. Fewer
   is better. If nothing is wrong, say nothing is wrong — a round that invents a fault to justify itself
   is worse than no round.

## The six axes

| Axis | The question | What failure looks like in a shot |
|---|---|---|
| **Hierarchy** | Can you find the one thing this page is for, in a second, without reading? | Title and section heading at the same weight · a date as loud as a title · three things competing at the top · the h1 lost under a full-width cover |
| **Rhythm** | Does the vertical spacing group what belongs together and separate what does not? | Every gap identical, so nothing is a group · a heading closer to the paragraph above it than the one below · a list breathing wider than the sections around it |
| **The one bold place** | The brief named exactly one. Is the boldness there, and only there? | Two accents at similar weight · a bold face *and* a bold colour *and* a rule · the named place quieter than something unnamed beside it |
| **The rut** | Is this the page this category always ships? | The five clusters: cream + terracotta · near-black + acid accent · hairline broadsheet · SaaS-card kit · tracked-caps eyebrow + middot meta + "→" links. Also: boxes inside boxes, and a card for anything that is only a list item |
| **Dark as designed** | Is dark a look, or the light one with the lights off? | Pure black or pure white in the pair · an accent that was right in light and is now either neon or mud · a surface indistinguishable from the background · a shadow still doing work it cannot do on dark |
| **Phone as designed** | Is 390 a look, or 1440 squeezed? | A masthead wrapping into three lines · display type at desktop size on a phone · a table or a code block scrolling the page sideways · touch targets under 44 px · a two-column grid collapsed into two unequal halves |

## The shape of a finding

> **&lt;candidate&gt;, &lt;scheme&gt;, &lt;width&gt;, &lt;route&gt;:** what you see, then what it costs the reader.
> Then the change, as a token or one rule.

Example:

> **C, dark, 390, `/posts/`:** the entry list's dates in muted amber compete with the titles in accent
> amber. Two warm tones at similar weight, so the one bold place is split. Drop the dates to the neutral
> muted.

## Two rounds, then stop

Round one after the first shoot, round two after the fixes. If something is still wrong after that, it is
a taste call and it goes to the owner with the contact sheet, in one line, unfixed. Do not open a third
round, and do not re-shoot to admire the result.

## What is not yours to judge

- **Anything a gate already covers**: contrast ratios, font budget, axe violations, layout shift, JS. If
  `check theme` is green on contrast, do not eyeball contrast.
- **Anything in `## Chosen`**: the brief overrode that rule on purpose and the reason is written down.
- **The content.** The specimen's words are fixtures. Judge what the theme does to them.
