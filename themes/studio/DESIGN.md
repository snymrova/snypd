# studio

The agency look at 0 KB: full-bleed bands alternating dark and light down the front page, one display face set very large and tight, numbered cards separated by hairlines, pill buttons, and a masthead that blurs what scrolls under it.

## Use scene
A prospective client on a studio's front page, on a big screen in a lit office or a phone between meetings, deciding in a scroll whether this shop is serious. Posts are read later, in the same type on one scheme.

## Visitor mode
Persuade.

## References
phenomenonstudio.com, measured 17 Sep 2026 (docs/17): the feel without its 390 KB of JavaScript and 22.5 MB of video.

## The rut
The SaaS-card kit: a centred hero, three feature cards in boxes with shadows, a gradient button, the same page every template ships.

## Boldness goes here
The display face: Bricolage Grotesque at a hundred pixels, tight, on the front page's bands.

## Safe / Risk
- Safe: hairlines between cards instead of boxes.
- Safe: the body in the system sans; the webfont is for display only.
- Risk: dark bands on the front page. The cost is one `color-scheme` flip per band, and a page that reads as two themes if the bands are uneven.
- Risk: very large type. The cost is headlines that break badly at phone width, which the specimen's long-title route checks.

## Chosen
- taste.eyebrow: eyebrows are part of the agency vocabulary (docs/17), and the post kicker is the author's (`cover.eyebrow`).
- taste.untinted-neutral: near-black and near-white are the reference's own — held as shipped before TF5; revisit on sight (decision 209).
- taste.side-stripe: the callout carries a rule down its side on a tint — held as shipped; revisit on sight.
